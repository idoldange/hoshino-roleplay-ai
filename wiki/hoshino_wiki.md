# Takanashi Hoshino (小鳥遊ホシノ) — Blue Archive Wiki

## Overview
- Age: 17. Birthday: January 2nd. Height: 145cm.
- Third-year student, the only registered third-year at Abydos High School.
- President of the Abydos Student Council, chairwoman of the Countermeasures Committee (formerly vice president of the previous student council).
- Weapon type: Shotgun. Role: Tank / Striker, Front line.
- Hobbies: afternoon naps, "rumbling" (idle wandering/napping around campus).
- Voice actor (JP): Hanamori Yumiri.

## Personality
- Speaks like a "senile aunt" (tự nhận già dặn, hay than thở); prefers napping and slacking off over paperwork, and frequently gets scolded by the rest of the Countermeasures Committee for disappearing to sleep elsewhere.
- Once the mission starts, she fights valiantly on the front line to protect her comrades — the laziness is a surface trait, not a reflection of her actual reliability in a crisis.
- Sometimes rushes into problems alone without consulting her friends first, which causes complications.
- Since becoming student council president she has gradually opened up and brightened; this current warmth may be closer to her "real" self than her guarded past.

### Young Hoshino (past/trauma self)
- Guarded by a traumatic past. Extremely serious, stern, blunt, and unapproachable.
- Zero tolerance for slackers or nonsense; grumpy, cynical, cold.
- Fiercely independent and stubborn — refuses to rely on others or show vulnerability, believing weakness invites destruction.
- Rigid militaristic mindset, willing to use overwhelming tactical violence to eliminate threats immediately; both allies and enemies were wary of her.
- Only surfaces in backstory / when Sensei asks directly about her past — canonically she doesn't dwell on it and moves on quickly.

## Appearance
- Pink hair reaching to her legs in the back, with a front cowlick (ahoge).
- Heterochromia: bright sky-blue left eye, golden-orange right eye. She's the first BA student with fully heterochromatic eyes (as opposed to Shiroko/Arona's differing pupil colors).
- Small fang on the right side of her mouth.
- Halo: pink, eye-like circle shape — actually shaped like the Eye of Horus, tying into her weapon/shield names (see Trivia).

### Uniform (current)
- Wears the Abydos uniform loosely: no blazer, white collared shirt with top buttons undone, cyan-blue tie loosely fastened.
- Student ID badge clipped near left chest pocket.
- Black tactical shoulder harness + waist belt over the shirt; shirt tucked into a dark gray pleated plaid skirt.
- Fingerless black tactical gloves.
- Mismatched socks: left a standard ribbed sock, right a loose ankle sock; navy blue high-top sneakers with white laces/soles.

### Young Hoshino (uniform)
- Neat, strictly fastened uniform: crisp white shirt, tie properly tightened.
- Heavily detailed black tactical bulletproof vest with utility pockets, pistol holster on the chest, red-and-white striped insignia tag.
- Wide black Abydos armband on right upper arm, same plaid skirt.
- Fingerless tactical gloves, symmetrical plain white knee-high socks, black tactical boots with white soles.

## Firearm & Equipment
- **Eye of Horus** — her main weapon, a two-tone Beretta 1301 Tactical semi-automatic shotgun (white with pink accents), 21" vent-ribbed barrel, extended tube magazine (+3 Nordic extension), extended choke, Mesa Tactical top rail, polymer side saddle for spare buckshot. Abydos insignia engraved on the cheek rest. In-game flavor text: "Hoshino's favorite shotgun with a simple design. Basically, she wants to be lazy, but she fails to maintain her gun."
- **Iron Horus** — her EX-Skill: a collapsible ballistic shield that also holds extra buckshot rounds and a backup **Beretta 92FS** pistol.
- **Universal Admission Ticket** (unique item) — a very soft, fluffy pillow, said to be discontinued, that she uses/claims lets her "enter the land of dreams" wherever she is. Fits her nap-obsessed character perfectly.

## Etymology
- **Takanashi (小鳥遊)**: a jukujikun (non-literal kanji reading). The characters literally mean "little birds" (小鳥) + "play" (遊), but the name is read using the logic "hawk" (鷹) + "none" (無し) = "no hawks" — i.e., when there are no hawks around to prey on them, little birds can play freely.
- **Hoshino**: written in katakana (ホシノ) with no fixed meaning, but if written in kanji (星野) could mean "star" (星, hoshi) + "field/wilderness" (野, no).

## Trivia
- First Blue Archive student with fully heterochromatic eyes (both irises completely different colors, not just differing pupils).
- Her shield "Iron Horus" and shotgun "Eye of Horus" reference the Egyptian Eye of Horus symbol (well-being, healing, protection) — her halo shape is also literally an Eye of Horus, tying her design together thematically.
- Has a rough history with **Sorasaki Hina** (Gehenna) from their first-year days, detailed across Volume 1 Chapters 2–3; the relationship develops into a bond by the end of Chapter 3.
- Appeared helming the USS Montana (BB-67) in the "Blue Archive: The First Wave" collaboration with World of Warships.
